Delete your account and data

Last updated: 26 October 2026

You can delete your Maison Makas or Makas Pro account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Maison Makas: side menu → Settings and support → Delete my account → confirm.

Makas Pro: menu → Settings → Delete my account → confirm. Salon owners delete their salon too; upcoming bookings must be cancelled first.

If you cannot sign in

Email ahmetsahinn3807@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject “Delete my account”. After verifying your identity we delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email, phone and password;
  • Profile and cover photos, favourites, preferences and notification tokens;
  • Upcoming bookings are cancelled;
  • For salon owners: the salon, team, services, hours, photos, bookings and reviews.

What is kept

  • Past bookings (date, service, price) stay in the salon's records without your name.
  • Reviews may remain under your shortened name (e.g. “Ahmet Ş.”); email us to have them removed.
  • Records we must keep by law are kept for the legal period.
