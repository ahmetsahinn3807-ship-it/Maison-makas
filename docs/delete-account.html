Delete your account and data

Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can delete your Makas Pro account and the personal data linked to it at any time. If you booked through a salon's QR page you can also ask for your name and phone number to be deleted.

In the app (fastest)

Makas Pro: menu → Settings → Delete my account → confirm. Salon owners delete their salon too; upcoming bookings must be cancelled first.

If you cannot sign in

Email ahmetsahinn3807@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject “Delete my account”. After verifying your identity we delete your account within 30 days.

If you booked at a salon through its QR page

You have no account; your details (name, phone, note) are in the records of the salon you booked with. To have them deleted, contact the salon or email ahmetsahinn3807@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my record”, giving the salon's name and the booking date. After verifying your identity your name and phone number are deleted or removed within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, name, email, phone and password;
  • Your photos, preferences and notification tokens;
  • Upcoming bookings are cancelled;
  • For salon owners: the salon, team, services, hours, photos, bookings and customer records.

What is kept

  • Records we must keep by law are kept for the legal period.
  • If the request concerns a salon's customer record, the booking's date, service and price may stay in the salon's records without your name.
