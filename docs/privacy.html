Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains which personal data is processed when you use Makas Pro, the booking app for barbers and hairdressers, and the salons' QR booking page, why, who it is shared with, and your rights.

App owner: Ahmet Şahin
Sancaktepe, İstanbul, Türkiye
Email: ahmetsahinn3807@gmail.com

Who is responsible for what?

  • For the accounts of salon owners and barbers, the data controller is Ahmet Şahin.
  • For the data of a salon's customers (people who book through the QR page and customers the salon adds itself), the data controller is the salon. Ahmet Şahin only stores this data on the salon's behalf (data processor). Details for customers: KVKK notice (Turkish).

1. Data we collect

  • Account (salon owners and barbers): full name, email address, password and, optionally, phone number. The password is stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Salon details: salon name, address, phone, description, opening hours, services and prices, team members' names and titles.
  • Photos: the salon's cover, logo and gallery photos and barber photos, if you upload them.
  • Salon customers' data: full name, phone number, booking note, chosen services, barber, date and time, price and booking status (confirmed, cancelled, arrived, no-show). It is entered through the QR page or by the salon in its calendar. Please do not write health information (allergies, illnesses) in the note.
  • Device: a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), device type (Android/iOS) and app language.
  • Preferences: theme, colour, language, text size and notification settings.
  • Technical records: when you open the QR page your IP address is briefly visible in the server logs of the hosting service (GitHub Pages) and the database service (Supabase). The QR page does not use cookies and does not store anything in your browser.

We do not track your location, show ads, use advertising IDs or analytics tools, or sell your data.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To let salons manage bookings and to prevent double bookings.
  • To send new-booking and cancellation notifications to the salon team.
  • To show the salon's showcase on the booking page.
  • To fix errors, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

3. Who can see it

  • The salon: the owner and the booked barber see the customer's name, phone, booking details and note. Other salons do not.
  • Everyone: the salon's showcase on its booking page (name, address, phone, description, services, prices and barbers).
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our behalf:
    • Supabase: database, sign-in and file storage; servers in the EU (Frankfurt).
    • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications; only the token and notification text.
    • Google (Gmail): sending account emails such as password reset codes; only your email address and the message.
    • GitHub Pages: publishing the QR booking page and these legal texts.
  • Authorities: only where required by law.

Some of our providers' servers are outside Türkiye (European Union and USA), so data is transferred abroad. These transfers are made in line with Article 9 of Law No. 6698.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and salon data are kept while your account exists.
  • Customer booking records stay in the salon's records while the salon's account exists. If a customer asks for a record to be deleted or the name removed, we do so within 30 days.
  • Notification tokens are deleted when you sign out, or automatically after 90 days without use.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos and tokens are deleted. For salon owners, the salon, team, services, hours, photos, bookings and customer records are deleted too.
  • Records we must keep by law are kept for the legal period.

5. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and each user can only access records they are authorised to see (row-level security). No system is completely risk-free; if a data breach occurs we notify the Personal Data Protection Board and the people affected in the way and within the time the law requires. If a breach affects data we hold on behalf of a salon, we tell that salon without delay.

6. Your rights

To access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing or use the other rights in Article 11 of the Law, email ahmetsahinn3807@gmail.com. If you are a salon's customer you can also contact the salon you booked with. Requests are answered within 30 days. If you find the answer insufficient or get none in time, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board. You can delete your Makas Pro account in the app under Settings (how to). See the KVKK notice (Turkish) for details.

7. Children

Makas Pro is for businesses and cannot be used by people under 18 to open an account. A parent or guardian books for a child and enters the child's details.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes are announced in the app; the date on this page shows the last update.
