1. Data we collect

  • Account: full name, email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Contact: your phone number, if you add it.
  • Bookings: salon and barber, date and time, services, price, your note to the salon, status (confirmed, cancelled, arrived, no-show) and cancellation time.
  • Photos: your profile and cover photo if you upload them; for business accounts, salon cover, logo, gallery and barber photos.
  • Reviews and favourites: ratings and reviews written after a completed booking, favourite salons and barbers.
  • Preferences: theme, colour, language, text size and notification settings.
  • Device: a push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), device type (Android/iOS) and app language.
  • Business data (Makas Pro): salon name, address, phone, description, opening hours, services and prices, team members' names and titles, and the name and phone of walk-in customers the salon adds to its calendar.

We do not track your location, show ads, use advertising IDs or analytics tools, or sell your data.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To let you book and cancel, and let salons manage bookings; to prevent double bookings.
  • To send booking reminders and new-booking/cancellation notifications.
  • To show salon showcases and reviews.
  • To fix errors, keep the service secure and meet legal obligations.

3. Who can see it

  • The salon you book with: the owner and your barber see your name, phone, booking details and note. Other salons do not.
  • Everyone: published salon showcases and reviews. Your name appears shortened in reviews (e.g. “Ahmet Ş.”).
  • Our service providers, who process data only on our behalf: Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage; servers in the EU, Frankfurt), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (delivering notifications; only the token and notification text) and Google Gmail (sending account emails such as password reset codes; only your email address and the message).
  • Authorities: only where required by law.

Because our providers' servers are outside Türkiye, your data is transferred abroad with the safeguards required by law (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

4. How long we keep it

  • Account data is kept while your account exists.
  • Notification tokens are deleted when you sign out, or automatically after 90 days without use.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, favourites and tokens are deleted and upcoming bookings are cancelled. Past bookings (date, service, price) remain in the salon's records without your name. Reviews may remain with your shortened name; email us to have them removed.
  • Records we must keep by law are kept for the legal period.

5. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and each user can only access records they are authorised to see (row-level security).

6. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion or object to processing by emailing ahmetsahinn3807@gmail.com. You can edit your name and phone in the Profile screen and delete your account in Settings (how).

7. Children

The apps are not for children under 13. Users under 18 may use them with a parent's or guardian's permission.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; important changes are announced in the app. The date above shows the latest update.
